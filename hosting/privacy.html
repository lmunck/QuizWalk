QuizWalk

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026. Applies to the QuizWalk app (also called Qualk) for iPhone and Android, and to qualk.dk.

QuizWalk turns a walk into a quiz. Someone creates a walk and shares a code; teams walk to each stop, answer a question and see the standings. We collect only what that game needs. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

Who we are

QuizWalk is made by NulKommaFire, an independent developer in Denmark, who is responsible for your data. Questions and requests: privacy@qualk.dk.

What we collect, and why

If you play

You don't need an account to play. The app gives this phone a random ID instead.

  • Your team: the team name you choose, how many players, solo or team, the team colour, and when you joined, started, finished or left. Everyone in the walk sees this on the standings, so pick a name you are happy to show.
  • Your answers: which answer you chose and how long you took, to work out your points.
  • Your location, only during a walk: the phone uses it to open each question when you reach the stop. Once you tap Start walk, your last known position (one point, overwritten as you go, not a trail) is shared with the walk's creator so they can see where teams are, and the app adds up the distance walked. Other teams never see where you are. The app asks only for "While using the app". When you build a walk, the app can also place the Start pin and stops at your location, and the Generate for me map opens where you are; only the pins you keep are saved, as part of your walk. Location is not used at any other time.
  • Reports you send: a blocked way to a stop (seen by the creator), or a question you think is wrong or offensive.
  • The list of walks joined on this phone, so you can get back into a walk.
  • A push token, if you allow notifications, so the app can tell you when a walk opens, when you reach a stop and when the results are in.

If you create walks

  • Your account: you sign in with Apple or Google. We receive an ID for your account and the email address Apple or Google hands over (Apple may give a private relay address). Only you see the address, in Settings.
  • The name players see, such as "Waiting for Anders", which you can change in Settings.
  • Your walks: the start, the route, the stops, the questions and their answers. Players of a walk see it once you publish it.
  • Generating a walk: the job's progress, and the times of your recent requests, to keep usage within fair limits.
  • A push token, for news about your walks.

Where it is stored

Everything above is stored in Google Firebase. The database (Cloud Firestore) and the server code (Cloud Functions) run in Google's europe-west1 region in Belgium. Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication, a global Google service. The phone also keeps a few small things on the phone itself (when a question was opened, so the bonus clock cannot be restarted, and answers that arrived too late); these are never sent anywhere.

Who receives what

Who What they receive
Other teams in a walk Team names, players, scores and times on the standings. Never locations.
The walk's creator The teams, their answers, their last known position after Start walk, and blocked-way reports.
Google (Firebase) Stores and processes the data above for us, as described under "Where".
OpenRouteService (HeiGIT, Germany) When a creator makes a walk: the route's points and the stop coordinates, to work out a walking route. Nothing about who you are.
OpenStreetMap (Overpass servers) and Wikipedia / Wikidata When a creator generates a walk: coordinates along the route, to find named places nearby and read about them. Nothing about who you are.
Google Gemini When a creator generates a walk or a question: quiz categories, difficulty and information about places (their names and Wikipedia text), to write questions. Nothing about you, your team or your location.
Expo Push Service (Expo, USA) Push tokens and the text of each notification (for example a walk's title, a team name and its score). It hands them to Apple or Google for delivery.
Apple and Google Sign-in, if you create walks. A check that the app is genuine (App Attest, Play Integrity). Map images for the area on screen (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android), and the next stop if you tap Directions.

Calls to OpenRouteService, OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia and Gemini are made by our server, not by your phone, so they never see your phone's address or your location. Some of these services are outside the EU; they get only what is listed here.

How long we keep it

Data Kept
Teams, answers and timing, last known positions, blocked-way reports 30 days after the walk ends, then deleted automatically
A walk's join code Until 30 days after the walk ends; then it stops working
The list of walks joined on this phone Each walk is taken off 30 days after it ends
Push tokens With your team (30 days after the walk) or your account
Question reports Until you delete your data or account. Counts without anything personal, such as how often a question was reported, are kept.
Your account, your name and your walks Until you delete them or your account
Times of recent generate requests A day at most
Server logs (errors, no locations) 30 days

A team that joined a walk that never opened stays in its lobby until the creator deletes the walk, or until you delete your data.

Deleting your data in the app

  • Players: Settings → Delete my data. This removes your teams, answers, locations and reports from every walk joined on this phone, and the list of walks. If a walk is still open, your team leaves it and the standings update.
  • Creators: Settings → Delete account and all data. This deletes your walks with everything in them (including a walk that is open), your teams in other walks, your name and your account. If you signed in with Apple, the app also asks Apple to forget the sign-in.

Both happen at once and cannot be undone. Removing the app from your phone does not delete anything on our side, but your walk data is still deleted 30 days after each walk ends. You can also write to us to have your data deleted or to get a copy of it.

Children

QuizWalk is not aimed at children as account holders. Children can play as part of a team on an adult's phone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and object to how we use it. Write to privacy@qualk.dk. Since players have no account, tell us the walk's code and your team name so we can find your team. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.

We use your data to run the game you asked for. Location and notifications are used only after you allow them on your phone, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

This website

qualk.dk is served by Firebase Hosting. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. Google handles your IP address to deliver the pages.
